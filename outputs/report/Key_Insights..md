## Key Insights

The analysis focuses on identifying sales patterns, product performance, and opportunities for business growth.

1. **Revenue Performance:** Total revenue and average sales value help evaluate the overall performance of the e-commerce business.

2. **Best-Selling Categories:** Comparing revenue across product categories helps identify which categories contribute most to sales.

3. **Top-Performing Products:** Product-level analysis highlights high-revenue products that may deserve greater inventory and marketing attention.

4. **Monthly Sales Trends:** Monthly sales analysis helps identify peak periods, declining sales, and potential seasonal patterns.

5. **Regional Performance:** Comparing sales across regions reveals strong markets and areas where sales strategies could be improved.

6. **Customer Contribution:** Customer-level analysis can identify high-value customers and opportunities to improve customer retention.

7. **Sales Distribution:** Statistical summaries and distribution plots help reveal variations in transaction values and potential outliers.

8. **Relationships Between Variables:** Correlation analysis helps explore relationships between numerical variables such as quantity, price, revenue, and profit, when available.

### Business Recommendations

- Maintain sufficient inventory for high-performing products.
- Plan promotional campaigns around periods of increased demand.
- Investigate underperforming products and regions.
- Use customer purchase patterns to support targeted marketing.
- Monitor sales KPIs regularly to support data-driven decisions.
